# Code of conduct

Sellbase is built by people with different backgrounds, languages (we work in Spanish and English) and levels of experience. We want everyone to feel welcome.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not accepted.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/jlgavel011/sellbase/security/advisories/new) (choose "code of conduct" in the title) or by email to the repository owner.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
